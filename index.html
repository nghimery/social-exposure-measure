Your child's typical school week

We want to know where your child usually is, what they're doing, and who's around them during a normal school week. Not a holiday, sick day, or summer week. This takes about 10 minutes.

  1. Drag across a stretch of time that looks the same (for example, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 3 pm).
  2. Answer a few quick questions about that time.
  3. Keep going until every hour has a colour. Tap any coloured block to change it.
